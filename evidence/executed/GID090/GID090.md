## P00 · ADMISSION

`Own(I10)` = the authority-bearing emission of a promotion receipt, and its refusal.
`NotOwn(I10)` = constructing candidates · supplying missing evidence · repairing defects ·
deciding truth. I10 adjudicates what nine gates produced. It does not produce.

**Coordinate lock.** `GID090 ↔ R08C06 ↔ IC10-I10`. Verified: `12(8−1)+6 = 90`.
Historical residue: the quarantined Atlas B epoch placed IC10 at GID135–144, which would put
I10 at GID144. That address is **not** used here; GID144 is SSN-M12, a different authority
(certification, not promotion). `KC27 closure ≠ IC10 promotion ≠ SSN solid-state certification.`

**Ordering law.** `I_{k+1}(C)` is admissible only if `I₁…I_k` produced lawful packets. A gate
that fails halts the chain — and the remaining gates return `NOT_EVALUATED`, which is a fourth
thing, distinct from PASS, FAIL and UNKNOWN. *Not evaluated is not a result.*

### The two blockers are infrastructural, not per-station

Aggregating across all five runs:

### Why GID044 promoted

It is the only candidate whose central claim is finite, stated exactly, and re-derivable rather
than retrievable. `verify_f01.py` re-executed seven claims independently — **12/12 checks
passed**, including the exhaustive enumeration over all 13,824 ordered row triples confirming
`#D_{4,top=1234} = 2`, the block-lift identity on all 256 cells at m=2, the closed digit form,
Latin-and-diagonal at m=3 (4,096 cells), and the set-valued nature of symbol-only decoding
(16 preimages vs 1 under row- or column-conditioning).

Per the corpus's own exception `Executed(x) ≻ Retrieved(x)`, execution re-derives rather than
repeats, so it counts as a witness where retrieval would not.

### Self-audit, entered against interest

The enumerator was written by the party submitting the candidate. Its *output* does not depend
on that party's assessment — counting qualifies under the harness law — and it is re-runnable
by anyone. But **"mechanically re-derivable by a third party" is weaker than "was re-derived by
a third party."** That gap is a residual, not nothing. It was entered as
`RESIDUE.GID044.VERIFIER` (S2, OPEN), which moved the verdict from `PROMOTE` to
`PROMOTE_WITH_CARRY`. The carry is not decoration; it is the honest name of what is missing.

## P06 · CONJUGATE AUDIT

What this node does **not** establish:

- It does not certify KC144. It certifies one carrier's finite combinatorial claims, with a carry.
- It does not make the corpus promotable. It makes the corpus *evaluable*, and the first
  evaluation returned four rejections.
- It does not resolve the independence problem. It **measures** it, and finds it universal.
- `STRUCTURAL_PASS ≠ MATHEMATICAL_PROOF ≠ AUDIT_PASS ≠ SOLID_STATE ≠ PUBLISH_READY.`
- The kernel's own construction is subject to its own finding: it was written and run by one
  party. It can refuse its author — demonstrated, four times — but it cannot certify itself.

**Adversarial check run against the kernel:** does it ever promote on structural evidence
alone? No — `I06.sigma_E_EXACT_requires_measured_witness` fires FAIL if `σ_E=EXACT` is declared
with zero MEASURED witnesses. Does it treat an echo as evidence? No — `evidence_class == "E"`
anchors are enumerated and excluded. Does compression ever precede promotion? No —
`qshrink()` returns `REFUSED` with `QSHRINK_FORBIDDEN` for every non-promoted verdict, which
fired 4/5 times in the live run.
