1. THE COMPLETED SHAPE
----------------------

V5 made the full crystal observable as one generated object. V6 does not
redesign that object and does not return to seat-by-seat construction. It
compiles the five open M12 gates into a single evidence-intake machine.

The architecture is now:

    frozen crystal C5
          +
    append-only evidence ledger L
          +
    dependency compiler D
          +
    M12 recomputation M
          =
    successor decision S

The crystal remains fixed. New evidence changes only an overlay:

    C5' = C5
    L(n+1) = L(n) append p

No packet edits an existing seat, edge, receipt, witness, or decision. Each
admitted packet receives a sequence number, the previous record digest, its own
packet digest, and a new record digest. The resulting ledger head is therefore
replayable and tamper-evident.


2. THE FIVE OPEN GATES AS ONE REPAIR VECTOR
-------------------------------------------

Define the production evidence state:

    E(L) = <b, d, r, x, i>

where:

    b = number of uniquely certified bridge subjects
    d = number of source-bound domain stations
    r = number of independently replayed station subjects
    x = number of unresolved blocking defects
    i = 1 only when IC10 has promoted the exact V5 candidate

The frozen V5 state is:

    E(L0) = <0, 86, 0, 1, 0>

The lawful M12 evidence target is:

    E* = <28, 144, 144, 0, 1>

This means the open work is not 144 sequential construction steps. It is three
parallel evidence waves followed by two authority-bound closures:

    R01 BRIDGE CERTIFICATION  ─┐
    R02 DOMAIN POPULATION     ─┼─> R04 DEFECT CLOSURE
    R03 INDEPENDENT REPLAY    ─┘          |
                                           v
                                   R05 IC10 ADJUDICATION
                                           |
                                           v
                                   R06 M12 RECOMPUTATION

R01, R02, and R03 are co-runnable. Rotation, algebraic generation, and frozen
address transforms identify the complete target sets immediately. They do not
manufacture the evidence needed to populate those targets.

4. THE COMMON EVIDENCE PACKET
-----------------------------

Every channel uses the same immune/reentry envelope:

    p = <
        packet_id,
        kind,
        subject_id,
        namespace,
        evidence_class,
        evidence_root,
        source_ref,
        replay_class,
        contradiction_class,
        repair_layer,
        trust_revision_witness,
        reentry_permit_id,
        payload,
        payload_digest,
        authority
    >

Admission is conjunctive:

    ADMIT(p,L) =
        known_kind
        AND exact_subject
        AND exact_namespace
        AND independent_evidence_root
        AND payload_digest_exact
        AND contradiction_typed
        AND matched_repair_layer
        AND trust_revision_witnessed
        AND bounded_reentry_permit
        AND verified_authority
        AND exact_authority_scope
        AND packet_unique
        AND evidence_root_unique
        AND subject_unique
        AND dependency_ready
        AND kind_specific_payload_valid

A failed conjunct yields HOLD and leaves the ledger byte-for-byte unchanged.

The envelope makes the immune law native to every future mathematical
successor. Contradiction typing, matched-layer repair, witnessed trust
revision, independent replay, and bounded reentry are no longer downstream
notes; they are admission fields.


5. KIND-SPECIFIC PAYLOADS
-------------------------

A bridge-certification payload binds:

    bridge_id
    signed two-phase commit digest
    transport evaluation digest
    return witness root
    PRODUCTION_TRANSPORT_COMMIT standing

Bridge certification changes transport standing only. It never promotes either
endpoint.

A domain-population payload binds:

    exact GID
    source object identity
    content digest
    typed carrier

Generated position, naming, route reachability, and rotational symmetry cannot
substitute for a source-bound content object.

An independent-replay payload binds:

    exact GID
    expected state root
    independently reproduced state root
    EXACT result

Self-replay, shared ancestry, copied outputs, and deterministic regeneration by
the original process do not count as independent replay.

A defect-closure payload binds:

    exact defect identity
    CLOSED result
    closure evidence root

It cannot be admitted until all bridge, domain, and replay subjects are
complete.

An IC10 payload binds:

    exact V5 candidate identity
    exact frozen V5 state root
    I01–I10 conjunctive PASS vector
    PROMOTED decision
    exact reserved successor KC144.V2::POPULATE_MATH144

IC10 alone may record this decision. SSN12 only observes and recomputes it.


6. TEST AND PRODUCTION ARE NON-INTERCHANGEABLE
----------------------------------------------

The compiler has separate TEST and PRODUCTION namespaces.

TEST packets may drive every transition in the dependency graph and prove that
the mechanism closes. Their production-effective projection remains:

    <0, 86, 0, 1, 0>

Therefore even a mechanically complete TEST ledger remains M12 HOLD and cannot
emit KC144.V2::POPULATE_MATH144.

PRODUCTION packets require EXTERNAL evidence class, non-test authority, exact
scope, verified signature standing, unique evidence roots, and packet IDs that
do not identify fixtures or synthetic data.

This is the critical separation:

    executable proof of the admission machinery
        is not
    empirical proof of the framework's claims

8. WHEN POPULATE_MATH144 BECOMES LAWFUL
---------------------------------------

KC144.V2::POPULATE_MATH144 is emitted only when the exact production M12
certificate exists. The seed then means:

    inventory the mathematical source bodies;
    freeze each source occurrence and its byte/content identity;
    distinguish claims, models, examples, conjectures, and empirical statements;
    bind every object to an explicit carrier and KC144 address;
    preserve contradictory, failed, and alternative branches;
    compile only typed cross-domain bridges;
    emit KC54 constructive/conjugate receipts;
    run IC10 without aesthetic promotion;
    independently cold-replay every successor;
    attach a bounded reentry permit before memory or reseeding;
    publish only what survives the complete evidentiary corridor.

Until then, MATH144 remains a reserved successor, not a current production
claim.
